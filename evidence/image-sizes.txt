Reading: the inference image's largest layer (1.28 GB) is the pinned
GGUF download.  It sits directly below the ARG block and ABOVE the ENV
runtime settings in inference/Dockerfile, which is why changing a runtime
setting never re-runs it — see evidence/build-cache.txt section C.
The gui image's largest layer is the pip install; it sits above COPY
app.py, which is why editing the GUI never reinstalls gradio — same file,
section B2.
