What this file is
-----------------
The acceptance test for route (b) of the P5 decision: instead of publishing the
adapter and asking the Space to reproduce the trainer's composite→text-only
re-key, we publish a MERGED text-only model that loads with plain transformers.
This artifact proves the merge produced such a model, and records one thing the
test was not designed to find: how much of the tuned arm's terseness is the
weights and how much is the sampler.
